# Terms of use

The multi-account plugins are free and open source under the [MIT license](LICENSE).

- **Your own accounts only.** Connect only Claude accounts that are yours, each on its own paid plan. Never connect an
  account that belongs to another person, and never share, lend or resell an account. Each account stays under its own
  agreement with Anthropic, which you accept when you sign in to it.
- **No limit is changed.** The plugins do not raise, pool or work around any usage limit. Each account keeps its own
  limits; an account that reaches its limit stops taking new pieces until it resets.
- **Your code, your call.** The workers edit your project in separate git worktrees, and your branch moves forward only
  when the project's test passes. Review what they did before you ship it.
- **No warranty.** The plugins are provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, as the MIT license says. Zoaftware
  is not liable for any loss that comes from using them.
